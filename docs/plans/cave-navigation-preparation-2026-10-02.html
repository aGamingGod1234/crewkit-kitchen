Decisions and limits

The main agent remains the sole strategic decision maker. It authors route legs, supplies, equipment and reactions; deterministic machinery validates facts and executes those choices. Existing collision checks and safe local detours are retained. Preparation guidance is not an enforced shopping list, and extra gathering must justify its marginal time and risk.

No whole diamond-pickaxe run, random-seed completion rate, autonomous hostile-survival rate or end-to-end model speedup is established by these focused checks. Unknown cave geometry and spectator-visible diamonds remain unavailable to the agent until observed. Guidance can improve one response and still fail in another; longer gameplay remains the next measurement after this local implementation.
