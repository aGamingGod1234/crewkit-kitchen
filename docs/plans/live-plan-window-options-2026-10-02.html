Both settings can be switched separately. The windows follow the selected agent. Closing a window leaves its task running.

Progress survives where it should

A carried iron pickaxe can be lost. A known Nether portal stays on the plan, with its last observation shown. Crafting and dimension-entry milestones remain part of the history.

The agent can revise the plan, reorder work or decide between recovering and replacing equipment. The viewer only displays those decisions.
