The central performance is unchanged. Warm DM, mining, and total move-mine medians were slightly faster in the current version. Cold first action was 0.364 seconds slower by paired median. One current trial had correlated slow responses across several operations, while baseline had separate cold-start and crafting tails. That distribution fits provider and model variance better than code-path overhead.

Ten values are enough to reject the earlier claim of a consistent 100 to 200 percent slowdown, but not enough to estimate P95 precisely. With nearest-rank calculation, P95 equals the maximum at this sample size. Raw evidence is stored in reports/native-tool-steering-ab-2026-08-23.json.
